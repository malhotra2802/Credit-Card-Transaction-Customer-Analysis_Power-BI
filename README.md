# 💳 Credit Card Customer & Revenue Analysis (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built on 10,000+ credit card customers to analyse revenue performance, spending patterns, customer segments and card usage behaviour.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The goal was to analyse credit card customer and transaction data, track key business KPIs and identify patterns in revenue, customer behaviour and card usage through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🤔 Key Business Insights

### ❓ 1. What is the overall revenue and transaction performance?

- Total revenue: $55M
- Total interest: $8M
- Total transaction amount: $45M

### ❓ 2. Which customer segment contributes more revenue?

Male customers contribute approximately $30M, compared to $25M from female customers.

### ❓ 3. Which card categories generate the most revenue?

Blue and Silver cards together contribute approximately 93% of overall revenue.

### ❓ 4. Which states contribute the most revenue?

Texas, New York and California are major revenue-contributing states, each accounting for approximately 24% as reported in the analysis.

### ❓ 5. Which transaction methods are most popular?

Swipe transactions contribute 63.1% of revenue, while online transactions account for only 6.2%.

### ❓ 6. What are the customer activation and delinquency rates?

- Customer Activation Rate: 57.47%
- Customer Delinquency Rate: 6.07%

## 📈 Headline Numbers

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Encourage high-spending Blue card customers to upgrade to Silver and Gold cards.
2. Introduce online spending offers and cashback to increase online transaction adoption.
3. Improve customer onboarding through reminders and first-use incentives to increase activation rates.
4. Explore growth opportunities beyond the leading revenue-generating states.
